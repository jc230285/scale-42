GIGA-42 signs MoU with Norðurþing for AI hyperscale campus at Bakki

50 MW first phase next to Landsnet’s 220 kV transmission line and the Bakki substation; FID targeted 2026, first-phase operations 2027.

Scale42 ·Aug 27, 2025 ·3 min read
BakkiIcelandNorðurþingGIGA-42Geothermal

GIGA-42 Holdings has signed a Memorandum of Understanding with Norðurþing Municipality to develop one of Iceland’s first AI-optimised hyperscale data centres at Bakki, Húsavík.

The MoU establishes a non-binding framework covering site allocation, zoning, infrastructure development, and permitting for a phased, modular campus. The target first phase is 50 MW, with the platform engineered to scale into a hyperscale build-out as anchor customer demand materialises.

Why Bakki

The site sits adjacent to Landsnet’s 220 kV transmission line and the Bakki electricity substation, with established fibre connectivity into the national backbone. Power delivery is dominated by clean geothermal generation, with co-location opportunities to recycle low-grade heat into adjacent industrial and community uses.

Timeline

  • 27 August 2025 — MoU signed with Norðurþing Municipality
  • 2026 — Target Final Investment Decision (FID)
  • 2027 — First-phase operations

Local impact

Construction is expected to support 100+ construction jobs with 50–80 permanent operational roles once the first phase is online. The project includes commitments to district heating reuse, potential co-location with aquaculture and greenhouse facilities, and vocational training programmes with local partners.

For Scale42 and GIG’s GIGA-42 joint venture, Bakki anchors the Icelandic end of a Nordic pipeline targeting low-carbon, geothermally and hydro-powered AI compute at industrial scale.
