Scale42 CEO: 'I'd rather build in Kristinestad than Texas'

A 235-hectare hyperscale-grade site, four 400 kV substations and a top-five Finnish municipality for wind-power generation.

Scale42 ·Apr 10, 2026 ·3 min read
KristinestadFinlandOstrobothniaWind powerVasabladet

In an interview with Swedish-language daily Vasabladet, Scale42 CEO William Tasney explained why his next major AI data centre is heading to coastal Ostrobothnia rather than the data centre belts of the United States.

The Kristinestad site, developed by Redeve Oy in partnership with the City of Kristinestad, reserves 235 hectares for hyperscale-grade data centre development. The area sits at the heart of Finland’s wind-power production — Kristinestad ranks in the country’s top five municipalities by wind energy output — with four 400 kV substations and Fingrid’s main grid backbone serving Western Finland.

Scale42’s planned investment, according to Vasabladet, exceeds the €1 billion commitment TikTok publicly announced for its own Finnish data centre project, making it one of the largest privately backed digital infrastructure projects in the country.

"I would rather build here than in Texas." — William Tasney, CEO, Scale42

The rationale, in Tasney’s view, comes down to four factors:

  • Wind-dominant electricity at some of the lowest wholesale prices in Europe
  • Grid headroom — four 400 kV substations and adjacent solar zones in development
  • Logistics via the 15-metre-deep shipway into Karhusaari harbour for heavy equipment
  • Stakeholder alignment — a municipality that has spent years preparing land and zoning for industrial-scale digital infrastructure

An advance reservation agreement was signed on 11 February 2025, with zoning and joint customer development now progressing alongside the City of Kristinestad.

"We are currently developing new data centre operations jointly with the city and customers." — Timo Heiskanen, CEO, Redeve Oy
"Kristinestad has attained an edge by identifying the future needs of industry." — Lauri Koskinen, Zoning Manager, City of Kristinestad

For Scale42, Kristinestad anchors the company’s Finnish footprint alongside its earlier projects in Varkaus and Kitee — a portfolio targeting low-cost, low-carbon AI compute at industrial scale on land that already has the power and connectivity to support it.
